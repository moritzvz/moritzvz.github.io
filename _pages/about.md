I am a researcher at the Institute of Information Systems and Information Management at Goethe University Frankfurt. My research primarily builds upon quantitative empirical methods and studies the development, deployment, and impact of artificial intelligence (AI) in business and society. I have published my research in leading journals of the managerial sciences, including *Information Systems Research* and *Marketing Science*. In my recent projects, I explore the potential of explainability, fairness, and causality in AI to solve business and societal problems.

I regularly collaborate with academic institutions, start-ups, and larger companies. If you are interested, please feel free to reach out via [email](mailto:vonzahn@its.uni-frankfurt.de) or <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/moritz-von-zahn" target="_blank">LinkedIn</a>.
